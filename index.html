04 / QUICK REFERENCE

What to look for

Thread

A logical work item that executes a kernel or shader program.

Warp / wave / subgroup

A group of lanes scheduled or executed together under a vendor-specific model.

Occupancy

How many warps or waves can reside on a compute block relative to its hardware limit.

Latency hiding

Switching to other ready work while one group waits for data or dependencies.

Cache hierarchy

Fast storage levels that reduce trips to high-latency device memory.

Bandwidth

The rate at which data can be transferred; distinct from latency.
