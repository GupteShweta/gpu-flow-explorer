Microsoft devices / Find My Device ↗Microsoft lost-device instructions ↗




Responsible recovery: Enable Windows Find My Device before a loss; it requires the feature and location to have been enabled and a supported Microsoft personal-account setup. For managed school or business PCs, use the organisation's authorised MDM/endpoint-management tools. If stolen, preserve purchase and serial-number evidence, contact the police and insurer, and do not attempt to confront or covertly track a suspected holder. A CPU model or processor ID is not proof of ownership.
